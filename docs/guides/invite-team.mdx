title: Invite your team
description: Share a project and collaborate in real time.
---

Every spi AI project supports **real-time collaboration** — multiple people can work on the same canvas simultaneously, see each other's cursors, and leave comments.

## Inviting collaborators

1. Open a project on the canvas
2. Click the **Share** icon in the top toolbar (the people icon)
3. Enter a collaborator's email address and click the invite button
4. They receive an email invitation (if email notifications are configured)

Collaborators with an account can access the canvas immediately. Invited emails without an account are queued until they sign up.

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| **Owner** | Full access — edit, share, delete, manage collaborators |
| **Editor** | Can edit the canvas, leave comments |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to the canvas and comments |

The owner can change a collaborator's role from the Share dialog.

## Discipline labels

The owner can add a **role label** to each collaborator — a free-text field like "Backend Lead", "Security Reviewer", or "Product". This label appears in the collaborator list and is visible to other team members.

## Real-time presence

When collaborators are active on the canvas:
- Their **avatar** appears in the top-right avatar stack
- Their **cursor** moves in real time with a name label
- Canvas changes sync instantly across all active sessions

## Comments and @mentions

Click the **Comments** icon in the toolbar to open the comments panel. You can:
- Leave comments on the project
- @mention collaborators by name
- Reply to comment threads

Mentioned collaborators receive an email notification if `RESEND_API_KEY` is configured in the deployment.
